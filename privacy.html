Aloy Bot Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 29, 2026

1. Overview

Aloy Bot is a Discord bot developed by Dragic for Fortnite Save the World utility features, including mission and reward tracking.

2. Data Collection

Aloy Bot is not designed to collect or sell personal user data for advertising or profiling purposes.

3. Epic Authentication

If Epic account authentication is used, Aloy Bot may temporarily process the authentication data required to complete login and maintain access needed for the bot’s features.

  • Access is used only for the bot’s stated functionality.
  • Authentication data is not used for unrelated tracking or resale.
  • Only the minimum required handling should be performed for operation of the service.

4. Stored Information

Aloy Bot may store limited technical or configuration data necessary for bot operation, such as server-related settings, feature preferences, or temporary session data. It does not intentionally store private message content or unrelated personal information beyond what is required for the service to function.

5. Third-Party Services

Aloy Bot may interact with Epic Games services and Discord platform systems in order to deliver its features. Use of those platforms is also subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the developer through the official Aloy Bot support channel or listed Discord contact.
